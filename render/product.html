产品全景：AI 全托管出海 × 链上信任

AI-Hosted Export, With an Onchain Security Layer

以前我们把能力分散在许多页面里。这一页按结构讲清楚：CloudSail 全托管流水线 → 八大模块 → 工厂只需要做三件事 → 核心公式 → Fideight 安全层 → 证据流。读完你就明白：AI 接管外贸全流程，工厂只需要发货。

Everything, structured: the CloudSail hosting pipeline → eight modules → the factory ships, nothing else → core formulas → the Fideight security layer → evidence flow.

01 · Pipeline

CloudSail 全托管：四层流水线The AI-hosting pipeline — four layers

L0 感知层

Perception

AI 眼镜现场采集 + 店铺订单（Shopify/Amazon/CSV）+ 邮件聊天多源汇入，机器看人看不过来的细节。

Glasses in the field + store orders + mail/chat — multi-source, machine-first.

L1 决策层

Decision

LLM 读单据、读聊天、读证据并给出建议；所有钱走确定性公式引擎，AI 理解但不碰钱。

LLM understands documents, chats, evidence and suggests; money math stays deterministic.

L2 执行层

Execution

建档、三档报价、15 国语种翻译、跟单催收定时任务、验货排期、物流跟踪——自动流转，人来拍板。

Profiles, quotes, translation, follow-ups, inspection scheduling, tracking — auto-flowed, human-approved.

L3 结算层

Settlement

发货即触发 Fideight 安全层：证据上链、托管放款、佣金分账，全程后台静默完成。

Shipping triggers the Fideight layer: attestation, escrow release, commission split — silently.

一句话 One line：业务员只剩两件事——拍板和见客户；工厂只剩一件——发货。
02 · Modules

八大模块，拼出一个完整交易Eight modules, one complete deal

1 现场采集

Field Capture

眼镜 App 拍摄、OCR 单据、语音速记。Glasses app capture, OCR, voice notes.

2 证据归档

Evidence Archive

IPFS/Arweave 归档 + sha256 指纹（scripts/evidence-archive.js）。Archive + sha256 fingerprint.

3 链上存证

EAS Attestation

哈希 + CID 写进 EAS schema，任何人可验证。Hash + CID in the EAS schema, publicly verifiable.

4 里程碑托管

Milestone Escrow

USDC 分段锁放，公式引擎确定性计算。Deterministic staged releases.

5 合规检查

Compliance Check

规则引擎预检国家×品类，MCP 工具化开放。Country × product pre-check as an MCP tool.

6 仲裁通道

Arbitration Lane

Kleros 陪审团 + 证据包直送。Kleros jury + direct evidence packs.

7 店铺接入

Store Connectors

Shopify / Amazon / CSV 连接器，真实订单流入驾驶舱。Shopify / Amazon / CSV connectors feed the cockpit.

8 收款结算

Payments

Airwallex 法币双轨 + 链上 USDC，佣金自动分账。Airwallex fiat rails + USDC, auto-split commissions.

03 · Three Moves

工厂只需要做三件事The factory ships — that's the job

1 发货

Ship

订单、报价、合同、翻译由 CloudSail 处理完，工厂拿到的是确定的生产指令。

Orders, quotes, contracts, translation — done. The factory gets a firm production order.

2 配合验货

Let us inspect

验货方戴 AI 眼镜上门，开箱点数自动留证；证据即信用，验完就是放款凭证。

A staked inspector captures on-site; the evidence itself becomes the release voucher.

3 收款

Get paid

货款在里程碑托管里，验货通过自动放款 USDC/法币分账——不用追、不用赌人品。

Escrowed funds release on attested milestones — no chasing, no trust gamble.

04 · Formulas

核心公式：钱的事不让 LLM 猜Money math is deterministic — never guessed by an LLM

公式 Formula含义 Meaning
release_i = amount × w_i第 i 个里程碑放款 = 货款 × 权重（如 0.3/0.4/0.3）Staged release by weight.
fee = amount × rate(0.3%–0.8%)协议费随规模递减Protocol fee declines with volume.
commission = GMV × rate − refunds佣金按净成交计提Commission on net GMV.
evidence_ok = (sha256(file) == onchain.hash)证据验证 = 本地哈希对比链上哈希Verification = local hash vs on-chain hash.
P(next_state | current)履约状态用马尔可夫链建模（见专栏首篇）Fulfillment modeled as a Markov chain — see the column.
红线 Red line：所有涉及资金的计算走确定性公式引擎；LLM 只负责理解（读单据、读聊天、读证据），从不直接决定钱。All money math runs through the deterministic formula engine; the LLM only understands documents, chats, and evidence — it never decides money.
05 · Security Layer

Fideight 安全层：钱与信的四道闸The Fideight security layer — four gates for money & trust

CloudSail 是主机，Fideight 是主机底下的安全层——对工厂完全透明，只在"钱要动"的时刻亮出来。CloudSail is the host OS; Fideight is the security layer under it — invisible to the factory until money moves.

存证 Attestation

Evidence

原文上 IPFS/Arweave，链上（EAS）只存 32 字节哈希。可验证、极便宜、永不过期。Payloads off-chain, hash on-chain (EAS).

托管 Escrow

Milestone escrow

USDC 按里程碑锁放（签约/验货/发货/签收），证据到、自动放。Staged release on attested milestones.

合规 Compliance

Oracle

禁运/制裁规则引擎在钱动之前拦截（如无人机→俄罗斯 = BLOCKED）。Rule engine blocks before money moves.

仲裁 Arbitration

Kleros

争议走 Kleros 陪审团，证据包三分钟送达，平台不当裁判。Kleros jury with ready evidence packs.

06 · Evidence Flow

一票货的完整旅程One shipment, end to end

下单 Order→合规预检 Compliance→30% 托管 Deposit→眼镜验货 Inspection→EAS 存证 Attest→40% 放行 Ship→签收 Sign-off→30% 结清 Final

每一步都留下可验证的痕迹；任何争议，证据包三分钟内送达仲裁。Every step leaves a verifiable trace; any dispute reaches arbitration with a complete evidence pack in minutes.

07 · Why Now

为什么以前的人失败了，为什么现在能成Why predecessors died, why now works

TradeLens（2023 停运）、we.trade（2022 破产）、Marco Polo（2023 资不抵债）——他们不是死在技术，是死在没有可信的现场数据和要所有人同时上船。现在：AI 眼镜让现场数据机器化采集，EAS 让存证成本降到接近零，USDC 让结算不需要银行联盟。详见 docs/why-now.md。

TradeLens, we.trade, Marco Polo died from missing trustworthy field data and consortium bootstrap problems — not tech. Now AI glasses machine-capture field data, EAS makes attestation near-free, USDC removes the banking consortium. See docs/why-now.md.

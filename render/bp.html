First wedge

芯片、设备、大宗品的中小贸易商与工厂——单价高、信任痛感最强、决策链短。展会现场价值感知最强。

Mid-size traders & factories moving chips, equipment and commodities: highest trust pain, shortest decision chain.

替代方案对比

Why not alternatives

贸易融资 SaaS 无链上担保；链上托管协议无现场采集与合规拦截。Fideight = 证据 + 资金 + 合规三合一。

Trade-tech has no onchain guarantee; escrow protocols have no field capture. We combine all three.
